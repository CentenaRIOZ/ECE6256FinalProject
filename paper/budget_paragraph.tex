Varying the budget $B\in\{256,384,768\}$ (Figure~\ref{fig:budget}) changes little: no condition gains more than 4 points from tripling $B$, and ordinary compaction is no better at $B{=}768$ than at 384 (Qwen 57.5 vs.\ 60.1\%, Llama 69.7 vs.\ 73.5\%). $B$ is a cap rather than a target, however: the task-focused summarizer writes short summaries regardless (Qwen 111--130 tokens at all budgets), so \ours uses roughly twice the memory tokens of ordinary compaction at a given $B$ (239 vs.\ 121 for Qwen at $B{=}384$). Comparing at similar \emph{actual} usage preserves the conclusion for Qwen: at $B{=}768$ the chronological ledger uses more tokens (295) than \ours at $B{=}256$ (209) yet scores 69.3 vs.\ 77.3\%, and pinning stays at or below 64\% at every budget. For Llama, ordinary compaction at $B{=}768$ (215 tokens) and \ours at $B{=}256$ (229 tokens) are tied (69.7 vs.\ 69.0\%).
